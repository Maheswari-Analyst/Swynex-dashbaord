E-Commerce Sales Dashboard | Power BI
📌 Project Overview
This Power BI dashboard provides insights into e-commerce sales performance by analyzing key business metrics such as revenue, profit, orders, customer behavior, and product performance.

The dashboard helps businesses make data-driven decisions by identifying sales trends, top-performing products, and customer purchasing patterns.

🎯 Objectives
Analyze overall sales performance.
Track profit and revenue trends.
Identify top-selling products and categories.
Monitor customer purchasing behavior.
Compare sales across regions and time periods.
Support strategic business decision-making.

📊 Key Insights
Technology category generated the highest revenue.
Sales peaked during seasonal promotional periods.
Certain regions contributed significantly to overall profit.
A small percentage of customers accounted for a large share of revenue.
